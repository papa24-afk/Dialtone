## Guidelines for Contributing Screenshots

When uploading screenshots to this directory:
1. **Resolution:** Capture at native window resolution (1220x800 or full screen) in PNG format.
2. **Privacy:** **Redact or sanitize any personal phone numbers**, real SIM IMSI numbers, or personal contact names.
3. **Accuracy:** Keep all modem diagnostic logs and version numbers visible to assist debugging.
